What syncs between Turkcell Pasaj and Odoo

Each record maps to a standard Odoo model. No custom models are added.

Turkcell PasajDirectionOdoo modelBehaviour
Orders → account.move Created as draft customer invoices, with order notes. Bulk push supported.
Customers → res.partner Created or matched by name or tax number. Matched contacts stay locked.
Products ⇄ product.product Created in Odoo or linked to an existing product. Custom names supported.
Stock ← stock.location Quantities from the selected Odoo warehouse update Turkcell Pasaj.
Payments → account.move Pushed invoices can be marked as paid in Odoo.

Connect Turkcell Pasaj to Odoo in three steps

1
Click Turkcell Pasaj in Odoo

An Odoo administrator installs this app and clicks the Turkcell Pasaj icon. Yengeç opens with your Odoo already connected.

2
Connect Turkcell Pasaj

Enter the API credentials from your Turkcell Pasaj seller panel.

3
Choose what syncs

Pick Turkcell Pasaj order statuses, the warehouse and invoice settings. Orders start flowing.

Requirements
A Turkcell Pasaj seller account in Türkiye
An Odoo company in Türkiye, with access to Invoicing, Contacts and Inventory
A Yengeç account. The sync runs on the Yengeç integration service.
Data shared

The connector reads and writes invoices, contacts, products and stock in your Odoo database through an API key the app creates for you when you click its icon. Turkcell Pasaj order data is processed by Yengeç, the integration service that runs the sync. No data is shared with third parties.
