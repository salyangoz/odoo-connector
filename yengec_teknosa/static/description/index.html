What syncs between Teknosa and Odoo

Each record maps to a standard Odoo model. No custom models are added.

TeknosaDirectionOdoo modelBehaviour
Orders → account.move Created as draft customer invoices, with order notes. Bulk push supported.
Customers → res.partner Created or matched by name or tax number. Matched contacts stay locked.
Products ⇄ product.product Created in Odoo or linked to an existing product. Custom names supported.
Stock ← stock.location Quantities from the selected Odoo warehouse update Teknosa.
Payments → account.move Pushed invoices can be marked as paid in Odoo.

Product Q&A

Connect Teknosa to Odoo in three steps

1
Click Teknosa in Odoo

An Odoo administrator installs this app and clicks the Teknosa icon. Yengeç opens with your Odoo already connected.

2
Connect Teknosa

Enter the API credentials from your Teknosa seller panel.

3
Choose what syncs

Pick Teknosa order statuses, the warehouse and invoice settings. Orders start flowing.

Requirements
A Teknosa seller account in Türkiye
An Odoo company in Türkiye, with access to Invoicing, Contacts and Inventory
A Yengeç account. The sync runs on the Yengeç integration service.
Data shared

The connector reads and writes invoices, contacts, products and stock in your Odoo database through an API key the app creates for you when you click its icon. Teknosa order data is processed by Yengeç, the integration service that runs the sync. No data is shared with third parties.
